For Brussels organisations

Don't miss the EU consultations that matter to you

The European Commission asks the public for feedback before it changes a law. Inspraak finds the open consultations that fit your organisation and shows who has already answered. If you want, it drafts a reply for a person to check.

How it works

  1. Pick your organisationSearch above, or open one of the examples below.
  2. See what fitsThe open consultations on your topics, how many days are left and who has already answered.
  3. Answer in timeRead a draft reply built from the Commission's own documents. A person checks it; nothing is sent for you.
