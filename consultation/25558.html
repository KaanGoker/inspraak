Evaluation of the European Environment Agency & the European Environment Information & Observation Network for 2022-2026

Full official titleEvaluation of the European Environment Agency (EEA) and the European Environment Information and Observation Network (EIONET) 2022-2026

closed Open for answers from 27 Jul 2026 to 21 Sep 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission is launching an evaluation of the European Environment Agency (EEA) and the European Environment Information and Observation Network (EIONET) covering the period from 2022 to 2026. The evaluation will assess the performance of both bodies in terms of effectiveness, efficiency, relevance, coherence, and EU added value against the objectives of the EEA Regulation and work programmes. It will also examine the EEA's role within the EU institutional framework, its governance arrangements, operational functioning, and the sunset clause mentioned in the Common Approach on EU decentralised agencies. An external contractor will support the assessment using desk research, performance indicators, and stakeholder consultations, leading to a Commission staff working document planned for Q1 2028.

  • Evaluates the performance of the EEA and Eionet across the 2022-2026 period based on better regulation criteria.
  • Examines the EEA's organizational structure, operational functioning, and governance arrangements for funding, budgeting, supervision, and management.
  • Assesses progress made since the previous evaluation covering 2017-2021 and considers the sunset clause from the Common Approach on EU decentralised agencies.
  • Analyses the contribution of both organisations to EU environmental and climate policies and legislation.
  • Utilises an external contractor study combining quantitative and qualitative methods, desk research, and performance indicators.
  • Includes a consultation strategy with a targeted survey in Q2 2027, ad hoc expert surveys in Q2-Q3 2027, and targeted interviews in Q1-Q3 2027, concluding with a synopsis report.

Affected, as named in the text: EU institutions; Member States; Scientific community; Civil society; Commission end users of EEA products; National authorities.

What is asked: Stakeholders are invited to share their views through the call for evidence to provide information and feedback on different aspects of the EEA's and Eionet's work, including performance, outcomes, and impacts in relation to the evaluation criteria.

Dossier summary from the portal

The evaluation of the European Environment Agency (EEA) and the European Environment Information and Observation Network (EIONET) is conducted in line with the Commission's Better Regulation and the Common Approach on EU decentralised agencies. The objectives of this evaluation are to: • analyse the performance of EEA/EIONET. • measure their efficiency. • assess the relevance and coherence of activities against the overall EU environmental policy priorities. • demonstrate the generated added value.
