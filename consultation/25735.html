Sustainability criteria for plastic recycling technologies

Commission Delegated Decision (EU) XX of XX 2026 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) 2025/40 of the European Parliament and of the Council for the purpose of establishing sustainability criteria for plastic recycling technologies

closed call for evidence, Ares(2026)7696337, ENV. Feedback period 14 Aug 2026 to 16 Sep 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission has published a call for evidence regarding three secondary legal acts under Article 7 of Regulation (EU) 2025/40 on packaging and packaging waste. These acts comprise two implementing acts and one delegated act that establish rules on calculating and verifying plastic recycled content, set sustainability criteria for recycling technologies, and define conditions for importing plastic recyclates or articles with recycled content from third countries. The initiative aims to make the minimum recycled-content targets of Regulation (EU) 2025/40 workable, verifiable, and enforceable across the single market. The rules are intended to prevent divergent national practices, support fair competition between EU and non-EU supply chains, and ensure credible environmental and health-protection standards. The Commission plans to adopt these acts by Q4 2026.

  • Covers two implementing acts and one delegated act under Article 7 of Regulation (EU) 2025/40 scheduled for adoption by Q4 2026.
  • Establishes a methodology and technical documentation format for calculating and verifying post-consumer plastic recycled content under Article 7(8).
  • Establishes sustainability criteria for plastic recycling technologies under Article 7(9), assessing performance, waste availability, energy use, and emissions.
  • Establishes an equivalence, verification, and audit methodology under Article 7(10) for plastic recyclates collected or recycled in third countries.
  • Aims to reduce reliance on virgin fossil-based materials, avoid fragmented national rules, and protect the single market level playing field.
  • Anticipates compliance, data collection, verification, and audit costs that may affect supply-chain actors, particularly SMEs.

Affected, as named in the text: Recyclers; Producers of recycled plastics; Converters; Packaging manufacturers; Brand owners; Importers; Distributors; Fulfilment service providers; Producers placing packaged products on the EU market; SMEs; Operators in third-country supply chains; Member States; Market-surveillance authorities; Scientific researchers, academic organisations, learned societies and scientific associations.

What is asked: Respondents are asked to provide views on the Commission's understanding of the problem and possible solutions, and to submit relevant published or pre-print scientific research, analyses, and data. In particular, the Commission seeks data on imports of plastic recyclates from third countries, including estimated annual import volumes by polymer type and the proportion of plastic packaging on the EU market originating from third countries.

Dossier summary from the portal

The Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) requires the Commission to create sustainability rules for plastic recycling technologies. These rules will help ensure that recycled plastic used in packaging provides the greatest possible environmental benefits.
