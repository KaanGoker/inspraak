Amendment of the Toy Safety Directive - Lilial, BPA, phenol and BIT

COMMISSION DIRECTIVE (EU) .../… amending Annex II to Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards allergenic fragrances in toys, bisphenol A, phenol and benzisothiazolinone

open on 5 Oct 2026 dir draft, Ares(2025)11233920, GROW. Feedback period 17 Sep 2026 to 15 Oct 2026 (open). Initiative on the portal. Topics: Single market.

What the Commission proposes

This draft Commission Directive introduces technical adaptations to the Toy Safety Directive regarding allergenic fragrances, bisphenol A, phenol, and benzisothiazolinone. It moves the allergenic fragrance Lilial from the list of permitted substances subject to labelling to the list of prohibited substances in toys due to its classification as toxic for reproduction. It also removes references to older European standards EN 71-10 and EN 71-11 for chemical testing methods, following the adoption of newer standards by the European Committee for Standardisation. Furthermore, the migration limit value for bisphenol A in toys is lowered to 0.005 mg/l based on an updated EFSA opinion and the new testing capabilities.

  • Bans the use of the allergenic fragrance Lilial in toys by adding it to the list of prohibited substances.
  • Removes references to obsolete European standards EN 71-10 and EN 71-11 for analysing chemical presence.
  • Reflects the adoption of new European standards EN 71-17, EN 71-18, and EN 71-19 by the European Committee for Standardisation.
  • Lowers the migration limit value for bisphenol A in toys intended for children under 36 months or to be put in the mouth to 0.005 mg/l.
  • Aligns the bisphenol A limit with EFSA's 2023 updated scientific opinion and the lowest achievable value with existing testing methods.

Affected, as named in the text: children; population groups.

Dossier summary from the portal

This initiative will introduce technical adaptations to the Toy Safety Directive which are necessary before the new Toy Safety Regulation starts applying. It will ban the use of the allergenic fragrance Lilial in toys, as this substance has been classified as toxic for reproduction. It will remove the references of European standards EN 71-10 and EN 71-11, as there are more recent testing methods. It will lower the limit value for Bisphenol A in toys to reflect the most recent testing methods.
