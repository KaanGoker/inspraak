Evaluation of Regulation (EU) 2021/821 (the ‘Dual-Use Regulation’)

Evaluation of the Union regime for the control of exports, brokering, technical assistance, transit and transfer pursuant to Article 26(4) of Regulation (EU) 2021/821

open on 5 Oct 2026 cfe evl fc, Ares(2026)2318110, TRADE. Feedback period 23 Jul 2026 to 15 Oct 2026 (open). Initiative on the portal. Topics: Trade.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission has launched an evaluation of Regulation (EU) 2021/821, known as the Dual-Use Regulation, to assess whether its rules on exports, brokering, technical assistance, transit, and transfer remain effective and relevant. Brought forward due to an evolving geopolitical and geoeconomic landscape, the evaluation examines the performance, efficiency, coherence, and EU added value of the current framework since its entry into force in June 2021. It focuses on identifying regulatory strengths, weaknesses, gaps, and potential administrative burdens for businesses. The findings will help the Commission determine if adjustments are needed to address new security threats while supporting trade.

  • Evaluation of the Dual-Use Regulation pursuant to Article 26(4) of Regulation (EU) 2021/821.
  • Assessment of effectiveness, efficiency, coherence, relevance, and EU added value from June 2021 to the present.
  • Examination of costs and benefits, potential administrative burdens, and simplification possibilities for businesses.
  • Review of the process for preparing and adopting delegated acts that update Annex I items.
  • Consultation activities including a 12-week open public consultation and targeted stakeholder consultations.
  • Support from an external study, cost-benefit analysis, market analysis, and literature reviews.

Affected, as named in the text: exporters; brokers; providers of technical assistance in the field of dual use; industry associations; national export control authorities; licensing, enforcement and customs authorities; non-profit organisations; research institutes.

What is asked: Respondents are asked to provide views, data, and input on the overall evaluation of the Dual-Use Regulation, specifically addressing costs and benefits, the reduction of administrative burdens, the simplification of administrative procedures, and how well the framework addresses evolving security, technological, and trade challenges.

Dossier summary from the portal

Regulation (EU) 2021/821 sets out rules to control exports, brokering, technical assistance, transit and transfer of dual-use items (goods with military and civilian application). Pursuant to Article 26(4), the Commission will evaluate the Regulation to determine if it has achieved its objectives, made a difference and if it remains relevant in view of the evolving global geopolitical landscape which may require adjustments to address new threats and reduce administrative burden for businesses.
