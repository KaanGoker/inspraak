Evaluation on the EU legal framework of markets in crypto assets

Full official titlePublic consultation on the review of Regulation on the Markets in Crypto‑Assets (MiCA)

closed Open for answers from 20 May 2026 to 30 Sep 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Banking and financial services.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission has launched a public consultation to evaluate the implementation and impact of the Regulation on Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA). This initiative fulfills the legal reporting requirements outlined in Articles 140 and 142 of the regulation. It assesses ongoing market developments related to crypto-assets within the European Union framework. The consultation gathers feedback from stakeholders to inform the review of the current legal framework.

  • Evaluates the implementation of the Regulation on Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA)
  • Fulfills reporting obligations under Articles 140 and 142 of the regulation
  • Analyzes recent market developments with respect to crypto-assets
  • Informs the ongoing review of the EU crypto-asset legal framework

Affected, as named in the text: Crypto-asset service providers; Market participants; Consumers and investors; National competent authorities.

What is asked: Respondents are asked to provide feedback and insights on the implementation and market developments related to the Regulation on Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA).
