Review of the Brussels Ia Regulation

Public consultation on the possible revision of Regulation (EU) No 1215/2012

open on 5 Oct 2026 opc launched, Ares(2025)9934618, JUST. Feedback period 1 Sep 2026 to 24 Nov 2026 (open). Initiative on the portal. Topics: Justice and fundamental rights.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission has launched a public consultation regarding the possible revision of Regulation (EU) No 1215/2012, known as the Brussels Ia Regulation. This regulation establishes uniform rules on jurisdiction as well as the recognition and enforcement of judgments in civil and commercial matters within the EU. While the application report concluded that the framework has been generally effective, it identified a need for targeted updates. The formal review aims to simplify, modernize, and clarify the provisions of the regulation to maintain a stable environment for cross-border disputes.

  • Review of Regulation (EU) No 1215/2012 on jurisdiction and the recognition and enforcement of judgments in civil and commercial matters
  • Assessment of the overall effectiveness of the current legal framework
  • Targeted updates aimed at simplifying, modernizing, and clarifying existing provisions
  • Ensuring continued stability and predictability for cross-border legal matters

Affected, as named in the text: Businesses; Consumers.

What is asked: Respondents are asked to provide feedback on the possible revision and targeted updates of the Brussels Ia Regulation to improve its application in cross-border civil and commercial disputes.

Dossier summary from the portal

Regulation (EU) No 1215/2012 on jurisdiction and recognition and enforcement of judgments in civil and commercial matters (recast) provides a stable and predictable framework for businesses and consumers in cross-border disputes through uniform rules on jurisdiction, recognition, and enforcement of judgments within the EU in civil and commercial matters. The report on its application concluded that it has been effective overall but identified the need for targeted updates to simplify, modernize, clarify its provisions through a formal review.
