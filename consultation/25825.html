What the Commission proposes

This draft Commission Implementing Regulation sets out the technical and functional framework for the HealthData@EU infrastructure established under Regulation (EU) 2025/327. It defines the architecture, interoperability requirements, and compliance checks necessary for secure cross-border access to electronic health data for secondary use. Furthermore, the regulation specifies the roles, responsibilities, and connection conditions for national contact points, authorised participants, and the central platform managed by the Commission.

  • Establishes a centrally coordinated architecture combining a Commission-operated central platform with components operated by connected entities.
  • Sets out rules for compliance checks covering legal eligibility, technical capability, security, and data protection before connection and periodically thereafter.
  • Distinguishes between major infrastructure releases requiring connected entities to adapt systems and minor releases managed by the Commission.
  • Defines specific connection criteria and compliance requirements for research infrastructures, third countries, and international organisations.
  • Details provisions for corrective measures, temporary disconnection in cases of non-compliance or significant incidents, and definitive exclusion for authorised participants.
  • Allocates responsibilities between data controllers and processors participating in the processing operations within HealthData@EU.

Affected, as named in the text: national contact points for secondary use; Union health data access service; authorised participants; health-related research infrastructures; third countries; international organisations; health data access bodies.
