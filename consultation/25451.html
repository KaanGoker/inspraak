ETS2 Financial compensation

Full official titleCOMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) …/... on financial compensation for final consumers of fuels and on allowances surrendered for emissions not covered by Chapter IVa of Directive 2003/87/EC

closed Open for answers from 17 Jul 2026 to 14 Aug 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Climate action.

What the Commission proposes

This draft implementing regulation establishes rules for Member States to provide financial compensation to final consumers of fuels who incur ETS2 carbon costs due to double counting or unintended coverage under Chapter IVa of Directive 2003/87/EC. It sets criteria for calculating compensation based on reliable estimates of CO2 emissions and the average auction price of Chapter IVa allowances during the relevant eligible period. The proposal also requires Member States to report annual data on compensated amounts to enable the Commission to adjust the Union-wide quantity of ETS2 allowances issued from 2029.

  • Compensates final consumers for incurred carbon costs resulting from double counting under Annex I or unintended coverage outside Annexes I and III.
  • Requires compensation calculations to be based on reliable CO2 emission estimates and average auction prices of Chapter IVa allowances over the eligible period.
  • Allows Member States to set eligible compensation periods of up to one calendar year (such as monthly, quarterly, or biannual).
  • Requires compensation to be provided within the calendar year following the year of the eligible period.
  • Mandates annual Member State reporting to inform Commission adjustments of the Union-wide quantity of Chapter IVa allowances starting from 2029.
  • Permits Member States to set compensation thresholds to limit administrative burden.

Affected, as named in the text: Final consumers of fuels; Regulated entities; Member States.

Dossier summary from the portal

This Implementing Act is adopted pursuant to the Commission’s legal obligation under Article 30f(5) and Article 30c(3) of Directive 2003/87/EC. It sets detailed rules for providing financial compensation to final consumers of fuels which face ETS2 carbon costs in cases where double counting or unintended coverage could not be avoided.
