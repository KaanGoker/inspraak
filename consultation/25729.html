Methodology for calculation and verification of recycled content in batteries

COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) …/... supplementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 of the European Parliament and of the Council by establishing the methodology for the calculation and verification of cobalt, nickel, lithium and lead recycled content in certain batteries, and the format for the documentation of such recycled content

closed reg del draft, Ares(2025)7729439, ENV. Feedback period 12 Aug 2026 to 9 Sep 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

This draft delegated regulation supplements Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 by setting out a methodology for calculating and verifying the recycled content of cobalt, nickel, lithium, and lead in specified batteries. The rules apply to industrial batteries with a capacity greater than 2 kWh (excluding those with exclusively external storage), electric vehicle batteries, SLI batteries, and LMT batteries. The act also establishes the format for documentation that economic operators must compile to prove the recycled shares recovered from manufacturing or post-consumer waste. Battery manufacturers are required to calculate these shares per battery model, per manufacturing plant, and per 12-month documentation period as part of their technical documentation.

  • Establishes calculation and verification methods for recycled cobalt, lithium, nickel, and lead in industrial (>2 kWh), electric vehicle, SLI, and LMT batteries.
  • Defines the format for documentation required to verify recycled content recovered from battery manufacturing waste or post-consumer waste.
  • Requires manufacturers to calculate recycled content for each battery model per year and per manufacturing plant.
  • Integrates recycled content calculations into the technical documentation under Annex VIII to Regulation (EU) 2023/1542.
  • Repeats the documentation calculation for each subsequent 12-month documentation period, matching calendar or bookkeeping years.

Affected, as named in the text: battery manufacturers; other economic operators.

Dossier summary from the portal

The Battery Regulation established targets for recycled content for lithium, cobalt, nickel and lead that several categories of batteries will have to meet to be placed on the EU market. Two sets of targets will apply first from 2031 then from 2036. In order to make these provisions operational this act lays down the methodology for calculating and verifying these recycled content rates.
