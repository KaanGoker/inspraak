AI strategy for cultural and creative sectors

Communication from the Commission to the European Parliament, the Council, the European Economic and Social Committee and the Committee of the Regions on an AI strategy for cultural and creative sectors

closed call for evidence, Ares(2026)6387101, EAC. Feedback period 27 Jul 2026 to 11 Sep 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Culture and media.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission plans to present a non-legislative Communication in the first quarter of 2027 establishing an AI strategy for the cultural and creative sectors. Building on the Culture Compass for Europe, the Apply AI strategy, and the AI Continent Action Plan, the initiative aims to define an overarching strategic vision that supports innovation and competitiveness while safeguarding cultural and linguistic diversity. The strategy is structured around three pillars: fostering collaboration between cultural sectors and European AI developers, promoting fair business models and ethical AI use while protecting original content and creators' rights, and equipping cultural sectors for digital transition through skills and EU funding schemes.

  • The initiative is planned as a non-legislative Communication from DG EAC Unit D1, scheduled for indicative adoption in Q1-2027.
  • The strategy builds on the Culture Compass for Europe, the AI Continent Action Plan, the Apply AI strategy, and complements the AI Act.
  • Action is based on Article 167 TFEU, aiming to address legal, economic, and technological challenges such as copyright licensing, unauthorised use of artists' likenesses, and reliance on non-EU technologies.
  • Pillar 1 focuses on fostering innovation and competitiveness through collaboration between cultural and creative sectors and European AI developers.
  • Pillar 2 aims to promote fair business models, ethical AI use, and the protection of European creation, original content, and linguistic diversity.
  • Pillar 3 aims to equip the sector for digital transition and leverage EU funding programmes including Creative Europe, Digital Europe, and Horizon Europe.

Affected, as named in the text: Cultural and creative sectors (CCS); Cultural and creative sectors and industries; Non market and market oriented cultural and creative sectors and industries; Artists; Creators; Professionals; Amateurs; Small companies and organisations; European AI developers; Citizens; Young people; Member States.

What is asked: Respondents are invited to give their views on the initiative regarding the future of creativity, access to culture, European creation, preservation and promotion of cultural diversity, and ethical innovation in the age of AI.

Dossier summary from the portal

A deliverable of the Culture Compass, the strategy will lay down a strategic vision for the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in the cultural and creative sectors and industries. The strategy will benefit culture in general and this European sector in particular, as well as citizens by supporting innovation, unleashing AI for European cultural sovereignty and promoting the development and use of AI to uphold genuine creation, cultural and linguistic diversity and inclusion.
