Evaluation of Regulation (EU) 2021/821 (the ‘Dual-Use Regulation’)

Public Consultation on the Evaluation of Regulation (EU) 2021/821 (Dual-Use Regulation)

open on 5 Oct 2026 opc launched, Ares(2026)2318110, TRADE. Feedback period 23 Jul 2026 to 15 Oct 2026 (open). Initiative on the portal. Topics: Trade.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission has launched a public consultation to evaluate Regulation (EU) 2021/821 on the control of exports, brokering, technical assistance, transit, and transfer of dual-use items. Pursuant to Article 26(4) of the Regulation, the evaluation aims to determine whether the rules have achieved their objectives and remain effective. It also assesses the legislation in light of the evolving global geopolitical landscape and new security threats. Furthermore, the evaluation examines potential ways to reduce administrative burdens for businesses.

  • Evaluation of Regulation (EU) 2021/821 on dual-use items
  • Assessment of whether the Regulation has achieved its objectives
  • Review of relevance in view of the evolving global geopolitical landscape
  • Analysis of new threats requiring regulatory adjustments
  • Identification of ways to reduce administrative burden for businesses

Affected, as named in the text: Businesses.

What is asked: Respondents are asked to provide feedback on the performance and relevance of the Dual-Use Regulation, its ability to address new threats in the evolving geopolitical landscape, and ways to reduce administrative burdens for businesses.

Dossier summary from the portal

Regulation (EU) 2021/821 sets out rules to control exports, brokering, technical assistance, transit and transfer of dual-use items (goods with military and civilian application). Pursuant to Article 26(4), the Commission will evaluate the Regulation to determine if it has achieved its objectives, made a difference and if it remains relevant in view of the evolving global geopolitical landscape which may require adjustments to address new threats and reduce administrative burden for businesses.
