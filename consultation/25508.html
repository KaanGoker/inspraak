Ship recycling - European list of ship recycling facilities (16th edition)

COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) …/… amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2016/2323 as regards updates to the European List of ship recycling facilities

closed dec impl draft, Ares(2026)796830, ENV. Feedback period 22 Jul 2026 to 30 Aug 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

This draft Commission Implementing Decision updates the European List of ship recycling facilities established under Regulation (EU) No 1257/2013 and Implementing Decision (EU) 2016/2323. It renews the listings for yards located in Denmark, the Netherlands, and Spain following five-year authorization extensions, while adding two recycling facilities located in India. The proposal also removes four facilities situated in Norway, France, and Türkiye due to non-renewal of authorisations, cessation of operations, or compliance failures. In addition, the Annex is updated to reflect modified maximum ship dimensions for a Dutch facility and to record distinctive company ID numbers and approval procedures following the entry into force of the Hong Kong Convention.

  • Renews the inclusion of Fornæs Ship Recycling ApS (Denmark), Hoondert Services & Decommissioning B.V. (Netherlands), and Desguace Industrial y Naval, S.L.U. (Spain) for five years.
  • Adds two Indian facilities to the European List: YSI Recyclers LLP (plot 59) and Shree Ram Vessel Scrap Pvt Ltd (plot 78/81).
  • Removes Green Yard Kleven AS. (Norway) and Recycleurs Bretons - Navaléo (France) following the non-renewal of their authorisations.
  • Removes two Turkish facilities: Kiliçlar Geri Dönüsümlü Maddeler Ve Metal San.Tic A.S. (operations ceased) and Avsar Gemi Söküm San. Dis Tic. Ltd. (failed inspection compliance requirements).
  • Updates Hoondert Services & Decommissioning B.V.'s entry with newly notified maximum ship dimensions.
  • Introduces distinctive recycling company ID numbers under the Hong Kong Convention and notes explicit approval procedures for Turkish ship recycling plans.

Affected, as named in the text: Ship owners (owners of ships sailing under the flag of an EU Member State); Ship recycling facilities (Fornæs Ship Recycling ApS, Hoondert Services & Decommissioning B.V., Desguace Industrial y Naval, S.L.U., Green Yard Kleven AS., Recycleurs Bretons - Navaléo, YSI Recyclers LLP, Shree Ram Vessel Scrap Pvt. Ltd/Shree Ram Shipping Industries Pvt. Ltd, Kiliçlar Geri Dönüsümlü Maddeler Ve Metal San.Tic A.S., Avsar Gemi Söküm San. Dis Tic. Ltd.); Competent authorities in Denmark, the Netherlands, Spain, Norway, France, and Türkiye.

Dossier summary from the portal

Owners of ships sailing under the flag of an EU Member State are legally obliged to have them dismantled (at the end of their operational life) in yards that are approved under EU health and environmental standards. This initiative updates the list of these approved recycling sites.
