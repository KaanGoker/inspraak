Endangered animal and plant species - suspensions of imports from specific countries

Full official titleCOMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) …/... prohibiting the introduction into the Union of specimens of certain species of wild fauna and flora in accordance with Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 and repealing Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/6

closed Open for answers from 30 Sep 2026 to 28 Oct 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

This draft Commission Implementing Regulation updates the list of wild fauna and flora species whose introduction into the European Union is prohibited, in accordance with Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97. It repeals and replaces the previous Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/6 to maintain clarity regarding trade restrictions. Based on the opinions of the Scientific Review Group and international developments, the text introduces new import bans for certain species from specific third countries while lifting prohibitions for others. It aims to protect endangered species by regulating international trade based on scientific and conservation criteria.

  • Repeals and replaces Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/6 with an updated list of import prohibitions.
  • Lifts import prohibitions for specific species from countries such as Guinea, Benin, Ghana, Togo, Indonesia, Côte d'Ivoire, Brazil, China, and Madagascar following Scientific Review Group conclusions.
  • Introduces new import prohibitions for species and specific specimens from countries including North Macedonia, Sudan, Benin, Burkina Faso, Togo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana, Laos, Indonesia, Cameroon, and Madagascar.
  • Reflects changes from the 20th meeting of the Conference of the Parties to CITES regarding species transfers and standard nomenclature.
  • Establishes prohibitions based on Article 4(6), points (a) and (b), of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97.

Affected, as named in the text: Third countries of origin; Importers of wild fauna and flora specimens.

Dossier summary from the portal

The purpose of this regular update to the EU ‘Suspensions Regulation’ is to introduce or remove trade restrictions for certain species of wild plants and animals from particular third countries, based on the mandate and using the criteria set in Regulation 338/97. Such restrictions are adopted or removed after consultation of the countries concerned and on the basis of opinions agreed by the expert group of EU Member States’ CITES Scientific Authorities (Scientific Review Group).
