10th report on economic, social and territorial cohesion

10th Report on economic, social and territorial cohesion, including report on the outcome of the mid-term review of cohesion policy programmes - Competitive regions for a Competitive Union

closed call for evidence, Ares(2026)6402490, REGIO. Feedback period 30 Jun 2026 to 11 Aug 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Regional policy.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission plans to adopt the 10th report on economic, social and territorial cohesion in the fourth quarter of 2026 as a communication accompanied by a staff working document. Prepared under Article 175 TFEU, the report evaluates regional convergence and divergence, the territorial impacts of national and EU policies, and ways to enhance competitiveness. It will also satisfy the reporting obligation under Article 18(6) of the Common Provisions Regulation by presenting the results of the mid-term review of cohesion policy programmes. The document will provide an analytical reference to inform negotiations on the next multiannual financial framework and future EU policymaking.

  • Adoption is scheduled for Q4-2026 as a non-legislative communication accompanied by a staff working document.
  • Fulfils legal reporting obligations under Article 175 TFEU and Article 18(6) of the Common Provisions Regulation (CPR).
  • Provides socio-economic analysis and data on regional disparities to serve as a reference for the next multiannual financial framework.
  • Includes a dedicated chapter evaluating the outcome of the mid-term review of ERDF, ESF+, JTF, and CF programmes.
  • Examines place-based policies, spillover effects from investments in less developed areas, and regional development challenges.
  • Draws on data from Eurostat, JRC, EEA, OECD, the World Bank, and academic research without requiring an impact assessment.

Affected, as named in the text: general public; national, regional and local authorities; civil society including non-governmental organisations and social partners; cohesion policy beneficiaries; managing authorities; Member States.

What is asked: The Commission invites stakeholders to provide views on its understanding of the problem and possible solutions, provide any relevant information they may have, and share evidence on how EU cohesion policy and national policies contribute to EU cohesion objectives.

Dossier summary from the portal

As required by the TFEU, the report assesses progress towards achieving economic, social and territorial cohesion in the EU. It examines whether regional disparities have been reduced and how national and EU policies have contributed to the changes. It identifies challenges and opportunities, highlights regional EU priorities and indicates territorial needs in line with the Treaty and the proposed next multiannual financial framework. It also covers the reporting obligation on the outcome of the mid-term review of cohesion policy programmes.
