Sale and repurchase of CBAM certificates

COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) …/... supplementing Regulation (EU) 2023/956 of the European Parliament and of the Council by further specifying the timing, administration, structure and level of fees and other aspects related to the management of the sale and repurchase of CBAM certificates, as well as the organisation and use of the common central platform, including the fees to cover the costs of the operation and management of the platform

closed reg del draft, Ares(2024)546926, TAXUD. Feedback period 9 Jul 2026 to 6 Aug 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Climate action, Taxation.

What the Commission proposes

This draft delegated regulation specifies the detailed arrangements for the sale and repurchase of CBAM certificates through a common central platform. Starting in February 2027, authorised CBAM declarants will purchase certificates from the Member State where they are established to meet their obligations under Regulation (EU) 2023/956. The act sets out rules to operate the certificate lifecycle across two distinct systems, the common central platform and the CBAM registry where certificates are held and surrendered. It also establishes the timing, administration, structure, and level of fees payable by declarants to cover the operational and management costs of the platform.

  • From February 2027, authorised CBAM declarants will buy CBAM certificates from the Member State in which they are established.
  • The sale and repurchase of certificates will take place on a common central platform established and managed by the Commission following a joint procurement agreement with Member States.
  • The common central platform operates distinctly from the CBAM registry, which manages declarations, checks, and the holding and surrender of certificates.
  • Member States will finance the repurchase of excess certificates upon request by authorised declarants through the common central platform.
  • To facilitate repurchase payments, competent authorities are deemed to give consent when approving repurchase requests in the CBAM registry.
  • Rules are set for the timing, administration, structure, and level of fees charged to declarants to cover platform operation and management costs.

Affected, as named in the text: Authorised CBAM declarants; Applicants; Operators and installations in third countries; Member States; Competent authorities designated by Member States; European Commission.
