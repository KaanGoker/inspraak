Implementing rules on registering in and reporting to the register of producers

Full official titleCOMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) …/... laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) 2025/40 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the format for registration in and reporting to the register of producers and the information to be provided in that context

closed Open for answers from 6 Aug 2026 to 10 Sep 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

This draft implementing regulation establishes harmonised formats and data requirements for registration in and reporting to national registers of producers under Regulation (EU) 2025/40 on packaging and packaging waste. It specifies four registration parts covering producer details, extended producer responsibility compliance mechanisms, certificates from producer responsibility organisations, and authorised representative details. The proposal mandates electronic submission via data processing systems that enable automated processing and bulk verification of registration numbers and certificates. It also provides for reporting packaging components and materials by weight, including composite packaging and plastic carrier bags, while accommodating integration with digital product passports and single-use plastics obligations.

  • Annex I sets out registration formats divided into Part A (producer details), Part B (extended producer responsibility compliance), Part C (producer responsibility organisation certificate), and Part D (authorised representative details).
  • Registration and reporting data must be submitted electronically through automated data processing systems supporting bulk verification of registration numbers and digital certificates.
  • Producers participating in separate packaging waste management systems within a Member State must register the packaging covered by each system separately in Parts B and C.
  • Reporting requirements require composite and multi-material packaging to be reported by the weight of each individual material, with separable components reported separately.
  • The format accommodates data reporting required to calculate annual plastic carrier bag consumption and extended producer responsibility requirements for single-use plastics under Directive (EU) 2019/904.
  • Where applicable, data from technical documentation for declarations of conformity or digital product passports may be used to provide the required information.

Affected, as named in the text: producers; producer responsibility organisations; authorised representatives; waste management operators; re-use system operators; public authorities; competent national authorities; manufacturers; providers of online platforms; fulfilment service providers; Member States.

Dossier summary from the portal

These implementing rules on packaging and packaging waste will harmonise certain parts of extended producer responsibility schemes for packaging in Member States, notably as regards the requirement for Member States to establish a register of producers and the obligation for producers to report into the national schemes.
