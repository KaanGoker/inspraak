Plants obtained by new genomic techniques: information requirements and verification of category 1 status

Full official titleCommission Delegated Regulation supplementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1388 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards information required to demonstrate that a plant is obtained by certain new genomic techniques and as regards the verification of category 1 status

closed Open for answers from 31 Aug 2026 to 28 Sep 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Food safety.

Grouped call: this publication belongs to 2 initiatives: Plants obtained by new genomic techniques: information requirements and verification of category 1 status; Plants obtained by new genomic techniques: applications for category 2 plants and their products.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission is preparing delegated and implementing acts to supplement Regulation (EU) 2026/1388 on plants obtained by certain new genomic techniques (NGTs). The initiative defines the specific information required from operators to demonstrate that a plant is an NGT plant, details the verification procedure for category 1 status, and outlines patent information and licence declaration contents. Additionally, it sets out methodology and safety assessment requirements for category 2 NGT plants and products. These acts aim to provide legal certainty, ensure smooth implementation, and make the NGT regulatory framework operational.

  • Supplements Regulation (EU) 2026/1388 through delegated and implementing acts.
  • Defines information required to demonstrate that a plant is an NGT plant.
  • Sets out verification procedures and information requirements for category 1 NGT plant status.
  • Specifies content for patent information and licence declarations accompanying verification requests.
  • Establishes methodology and information requirements for category 2 NGT food, feed, and environmental risk assessments.
  • Provides adapted arrangements for analytical method performance requirements.

Affected, as named in the text: operators; competent authorities; Member States; European Food Safety Authority; small and medium-sized enterprises; breeders; farmers; consumers; scientific researchers; academic organisations; learned societies; scientific associations; organic sector.

What is asked: Feedback on the Commission's understanding of the problem and possible solutions, as well as relevant information, data, published and pre-print scientific research, analyses, and examples of NGT plants with information on molecular characterisation and introduced traits.

Dossier summary from the portal

This initiative supplements Regulation (EU) 2026/1388 on plants produced by certain new genomic techniques (NGTs) by defining the information required from operators to demonstrate that a plant is an NGT plant and for the verification of plants and products equivalent to conventional ones (category 1). It is intended to provide clarity and predictability for operators on how to prepare verification requests and how to submit information on patents. It will also support Member States and EFSA in the implementation of Regulation (EU) 2026/1388.
