The Union Position for the World Radiocommunication Conference 2027

Full official titleProposal for a Council Decision on the position to be taken on behalf of the European Union in the ITU World Radiocommunication Conference 2027 (WRC-27)

closed Open for answers from 14 Jul 2026 to 11 Sep 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Digital economy and society.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission plans to propose a Council decision establishing the European Union position for the ITU World Radiocommunication Conference 2027 (WRC-27). Because the EU holds only observer status while Member States enjoy full membership and voting rights, Member States must jointly represent the agreed Union position on agenda items affecting EU policies, legislation, or programmes. The initiative aims to ensure the principle of unity in external negotiations on matters falling under EU competence, based on Article 218(9) TFEU. The preparatory work will take into account technical studies from the European Conference of Postal and Telecommunications Administrations (CEPT) and recommendations from the Radio Spectrum Policy Group (RSPG).

  • The initiative will propose a binding Council decision under Article 218(9) TFEU establishing the EU position for WRC-27, scheduled for 18 October to 12 November 2027.
  • The EU holds only observer status in the ITU, meaning EU Member States must jointly represent and defend the common EU position in the negotiations.
  • The Council decision will cover only WRC-27 agenda items that fall under EU competence, including decisions affecting EU spectrum law, policies, and programmes such as Copernicus and IRIS2.
  • WRC-27 decisions may affect connectivity, mobile phone links to satellite spectrum, maritime and aeronautical communications, climate monitoring, space weather, and air traffic safety spectrum.
  • Preparations will incorporate technical work and common positions from the CEPT and an RSPG opinion expected to be adopted in December 2026.
  • WRC-27 will also decide the agenda for the subsequent WRC in 2031 (WRC-31), which must align with current and future EU policies.

Affected, as named in the text: EU Member States; People and businesses; Public- and private-sector participants.

What is asked: The document is a call for evidence for an initiative without an impact assessment, but does not explicitly specify particular consultation questions or consultation topics for respondents to address.

Dossier summary from the portal

The initiative aims at proposing a Union position to guide EU Member States when negotiating on behalf of the Union at the World Radiocommunication Conference 2027 (WRC-27), organised by the International Telecommunication Union from 18 October to 12 November 2027. A common Union position is needed since the Conference is expected to take multiple decisions that might affect EU law, policies or programmes relying on radio spectrum. This coordinated process is consistent with what was done for the WRC-19 and WRC-23.
