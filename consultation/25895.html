Review of the International Procurement Instrument

Full official titleInternational Procurement Instrument review

open on 5 Oct 2026 Open for answers from 8 Sep 2026 to 1 Dec 2026 (open). Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Trade.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission has launched a consultation to review the scope, functioning, and efficiency of the International Procurement Instrument (IPI). The initiative evaluates all aspects of the current instrument, including investigation processes, consultations with third countries, and the conditions for imposing measures. It analyses the effectiveness of the current rules to identify potential areas for improvement. Stakeholders are invited to share their views on how the instrument operates in practice.

  • Review of the scope, functioning, and efficiency of the International Procurement Instrument
  • Assessment of IPI investigation initiation and processes
  • Evaluation of consultations with third countries
  • Review of conditions, thresholds, types, and exceptions for IPI measures
  • Analysis of the implementation and effectiveness of current measures

Affected, as named in the text: Third countries; Procurement entities; Economic operators.

What is asked: Respondents are asked to comment on the scope, functioning, and efficiency of the International Procurement Instrument, including investigation processes, third country consultations, and potential improvements to the instrument.

Dossier summary from the portal

The initiative aims to review the scope, functioning and efficiency of the International Procurement Instrument (IPI). It will focus on the assessment of all aspects of the IPI including the initiation of IPI investigations, the investigation process, consultations with third countries, conditions for the imposition of IPI measures, thresholds, types of measures, exceptions and the implementation of the IPI measures. It will analyse the efficiency and effectiveness of the current instrument and could contain suggestions for its improvement.
