Housing simplification package

closed Open for answers from 1 Jul 2026 to 30 Sep 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Single market.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission is preparing a Housing Simplification Package to address the EU housing affordability crisis driven by regulatory complexity and supply-demand mismatches. The initiative aims to map and assess the impact of EU legislation on housing supply and affordability, while disentangling it from national, regional and local rules. Planned as both legislative and non-legislative measures for Q2 2027, it seeks to reduce administrative burdens, accelerate permitting, and collect best practices in simplification.

  • Addresses regulatory burdens and administrative complexity affecting housing supply and affordability across EU, national, regional, and local levels.
  • Forms one of the ten priorities of the European Affordable Housing Plan and the Regulatory Deep Cleaning Action Plan.
  • Covers multiple sectoral policies including procurement, finance, taxation, planning, land use, circular economy, environment, energy efficiency, construction products, and skills.
  • Will be executed through mapping and assessing relevant EU legislation and identifying concrete simplification opportunities and best practices.
  • Expected to reduce economic costs, shorten permit-granting and renovation processes, lower housing prices and rents, and increase market openness for smaller actors.

Affected, as named in the text: national, regional and local authorities in Member States; social and affordable housing providers; urban planners; professionals from the construction ecosystem and real estate; civil society organisations; financial institutions; property owners; tenant associations.

What is asked: Respondents are asked to provide views on the Commission's problem understanding and possible solutions, examples of how EU legislation impacts housing supply, practical suggestions to reduce delays and costs in EU and domestic legislation, and ideas for increasing productivity in construction and renovation.

Dossier summary from the portal

Based on a comprehensive mapping of relevant EU legislation and initiatives and their combined impact on housing supply and affordability, the package will address regulatory simplification opportunities for the housing sector, while respecting wider policy goals. The package will also cover implementation of EU legislation in the Member States and best practices of simplification at national, regional and local level. It is one of the ten priorities of the European affordable housing plan and one of its key measures to boost housing supply.
