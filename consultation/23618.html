Ecodesign requirements for iron and steel products

Full official titlePublic consultation for the impact assessment on ecodesign requirements for iron and steel products

closed Open for answers from 20 May 2026 to 12 Aug 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Single market.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission is holding a public consultation for an impact assessment on ecodesign requirements for iron and steel products. Making the manufacturing and use of steel more sustainable is identified as a central objective under the EU Clean Industrial Deal and the Steel and Metal Action Plan. In parallel, the proposed Industrial Accelerator Act introduces low-carbon requirements for steel in public procurement and public support schemes. Under this specific initiative, the Commission plans to define low-carbon steel and establish ecodesign requirements to foster sustainable and circular design. It also aims to improve access to information concerning the sustainability and circular production of iron and steel products.

  • Supports objectives of the EU Clean Industrial Deal and the Steel and Metal Action Plan to make steel manufacturing and use more sustainable.
  • Complements the proposed Industrial Accelerator Act, which introduces low-carbon requirements for steel in public procurement and public support schemes.
  • Establishes an official definition for low-carbon steel.
  • Sets ecodesign requirements to promote sustainable and circular design of iron and steel products.
  • Aims to improve access to information on the sustainability and circular production of iron and steel products.

What is asked: Respondents are invited to provide feedback via the consultation questionnaire on ecodesign requirements for iron and steel products and the definition of low-carbon steel to support the European Commission's impact assessment.

Dossier summary from the portal

Making the manufacturing and use of steel more sustainable is a central goal of the EU Clean Industrial Deal and the Steel and Metal Action Plan. The proposed Industrial Accelerator Act introduces low-carbon requirements for steel in public procurement and public support schemes. This initiative will define low-carbon steel and set ecodesign requirements to promote the sustainable and circular design of iron and steel products and improve access to information relevant to their sustainability and circular production.
