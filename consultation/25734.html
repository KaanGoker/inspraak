Conditions for importing recycled materials into the EU for use in packaging

Full official titleCommission Implementing Decision (EU) XX of XX 2026 on the methodology for admitting recycled content from third countries, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2025/40 of the European Parliament and of the Council

closed Open for answers from 14 Aug 2026 to 16 Sep 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission has published a call for evidence covering three acts under Article 7 of Regulation (EU) 2025/40 on packaging and packaging waste. The initiative encompasses two implementing acts and one delegated act addressing the calculation and verification of plastic recycled content, sustainability criteria for recycling technologies, and conditions for importing plastic recyclates or articles with recycled plastic from third countries. Specifically, the implementing act under Article 7(10) will set out a methodology to assess, verify and certify equivalence where post-consumer plastic waste is collected or recycled outside the EU. The Commission plans to adopt these acts by 31 December 2026 to ensure recycled-content targets are workable, verifiable and enforceable.

  • The initiative prepares two implementing acts and one delegated act under Article 7 of Regulation (EU) 2025/40, with an indicative adoption deadline of 31 December 2026.
  • An implementing act under Article 7(8) will set methodology for calculating and verifying post-consumer recycled plastic content within the EU and establish technical documentation formats.
  • A delegated act under Article 7(9) will set sustainability criteria for plastic recycling technologies based on economic and environmental performance, output quality, waste availability, energy use and emissions.
  • An implementing act under Article 7(10) will establish rules to assess, verify and certify equivalence, including via third-party audits, for recycled content collected or recycled in third countries.
  • The harmonised rules aim to prevent divergent national practices, support single-market circulation, protect human health and the environment, and secure a level playing field between EU and non-EU operators.
  • Economic operators may face costs related to data collection, record keeping, verification, certification, third-party audits and the adaptation of IT systems, with potential disproportionate impacts on SMEs.

Affected, as named in the text: Recyclers; Producers of recycled plastics; Converters; Packaging manufacturers; Brand owners; Importers; Distributors; Fulfilment service providers; Producers placing packaged products on the EU market; SMEs; Operators in third-country supply chains; Scientific researchers, academic organisations, learned societies and scientific associations.

What is asked: The Commission invites feedback on its understanding of the problem and possible solutions, as well as published and pre-print scientific research, analyses and data. Specifically, it requests evidence on: (1) data on imports of plastic recyclates, including estimated annual import volumes of recycled content by polymer type, the proportion of plastic packaging on the EU market originating from third countries, and which third countries serve as the main sources; and (2) verification, certification or chain-of-custody schemes currently used to verify the origin, traceability and recycled content of post-consumer plastic waste.
