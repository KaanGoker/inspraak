Stress test of the Birds and Habitats Directives

Open public consultation as part of the stress test of the Birds and Habitats Directives

closed opc launched, NatureDirectivesPublicConsultationStressTest2026, ENV. Feedback period 12 May 2026 to 10 Aug 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission has launched an open public consultation as part of a stress test of the Birds and Habitats Directives. This exercise forms part of the Commission's wider simplification initiative, which seeks to enhance EU competitiveness while safeguarding economic, social, and environmental goals. The stress test intends to explore opportunities for delivering the directives' objectives more cost-effectively and without lowering their standards. Furthermore, it aims to pinpoint potential areas for simplification and reduce unnecessary administrative burden.

  • Open public consultation launched under the stress test of the Birds and Habitats Directives (nature directives).
  • Forms part of the European Commission's wider initiative on simplification.
  • Aims to enhance EU competitiveness while safeguarding economic, social, and environmental goals.
  • Seeks opportunities to achieve the directives' objectives more cost-effectively while maintaining them.
  • Aims to identify potential areas for simplification.
  • Aims to identify opportunities to reduce unnecessary administrative burden.

What is asked: Respondents are invited to provide views on potential areas for simplification, opportunities to achieve the objectives of the Birds and Habitats Directives more cost-effectively while maintaining those objectives, and ways to reduce unnecessary administrative burden.
