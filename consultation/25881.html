Freezing of meat of domestic ungulates

Full official titleCOMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) …/... amending Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards hygiene rules on storage conditions of fresh meat of domestic ungulates before freezing

closed Open for answers from 7 Sep 2026 to 5 Oct 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Food safety.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission proposes to amend Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 to update hygiene rules on the storage conditions of fresh meat of domestic ungulates before freezing. Based on a scientific opinion by EFSA, the draft regulation replaces the general requirement of freezing without undue delay with more specific time and temperature combinations. Food business operators may now freeze meat within 6 days at a maximum of 7 degrees Celsius, within 14 days at a maximum of 3 degrees Celsius, or under alternative scientifically supported conditions approved by competent authorities. The amendments do not apply to meat from even-toed farmed game mammals due to a lack of available data.

  • Replaces the requirement for freezing without undue delay for domestic ungulates with specific time and temperature criteria.
  • Allows freezing within 6 days of slaughter if stored and transported at a maximum of 7 degrees Celsius.
  • Allows freezing within 14 days of slaughter if stored and transported at a maximum of 3 degrees Celsius.
  • Permits alternative time and temperature conditions if food business operators provide scientific evidence demonstrating safety to competent authorities.
  • Excludes even-toed farmed game mammals from these new storage conditions, maintaining the requirement for freezing without undue delay.
  • Takes into account a scientific opinion adopted by EFSA on 3 December 2025 regarding the microbiological safety of ungulates meat.

Affected, as named in the text: food business operators; competent authorities.

What is asked: Feedback on the draft Commission Delegated Regulation amending Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 regarding hygiene rules on storage conditions of fresh meat of domestic ungulates before freezing.

Dossier summary from the portal

Based on a new EFSA opinion on the storage of meat of domestic ungulates before freezing, alternative, equivalent storage conditions are proposed e.g. for a period up to 14 days at a lower temperature or when complying with a process hygiene criterion before freezing. More flexibility thereby is provided to food business operators following a risk-based approach.
