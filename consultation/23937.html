Digital Networks Act

Proposal for a REGULATION OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL on the procedure for authorisation of systems providing mobile satellite services using the harmonised 2 GHz frequency band and repealing Decision No 626/2008/EC

closed prop reg, Ares(2025)4305261, CNECT. Feedback period 2 Jul 2026 to 7 Sep 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Digital economy and society.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission proposes a regulation to establish a common EU-level procedure for the selection and authorisation of systems providing mobile satellite services (MSS) in the harmonised 2 GHz frequency band, repealing Decision No 626/2008/EC ahead of the expiry of existing rights in May 2027. The proposed framework empowers the Commission to select operators and grant spectrum rights under uniform Union conditions, replacing fragmented national authorisation regimes. It specifically targets the deployment of Low Earth Orbit (LEO) constellations and satellite Direct-to-Device (D2D) connectivity to provide voice, SMS, and broadband directly to unmodified end-user devices. Furthermore, the initiative allocates spectrum to support secure governmental communications under the IRIS² programme and allows the Commission to grant transitional extensions to current right holders to maintain service continuity during the selection procedure.

  • Repeals Decision No 626/2008/EC and establishes a centralised Union selection and authorisation regime for the 2 GHz MSS frequency band.
  • Grants spectrum rights at EU level under harmonised conditions, anticipating the satellite authorisation framework envisioned in the Digital Networks Act.
  • Promotes satellite Direct-to-Device (D2D) connectivity and multi-orbit Low Earth Orbit (LEO) constellations integrated with terrestrial networks.
  • Reserves part of the 2 GHz MSS spectrum to enhance secure governmental satellite communications within the IRIS² programme framework.
  • Allows temporary, conditional extensions of existing spectrum rights upon request from current holders to ensure service continuity during the selection process.
  • Harmonises authorisation rules, procedures, and fees across Member States to remove single market fragmentation and support EU technological sovereignty.

Affected, as named in the text: Current holders of rights of use of spectrum in the 2 GHz MSS band; Undertakings providing mobile satellite services and satellite systems; Space sector and satellite equipment manufacturers; Member States and national regulatory authorities; Governmental actors and emergency response bodies; End-users of mobile devices (citizens and businesses).

Dossier summary from the portal

The initiative aims at improving access to secure, fast, and reliable connectivity for the transition towards cloud-based infrastructure and Artificial Intelligence. To this end, it aims to help boost secure high-speed broadband, both fixed and wireless, and incentivise and encourage investments in digital infrastructure. It builds on the Commission’s White Paper: “How to master Europe’s digital infrastructure needs” adopted on 21 February 2024.
