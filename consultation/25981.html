Revision of EU rules on public procurement

Full official titleProposal for a REGULATION OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL on public contracts and concessions, repealing Directives 2014/23/EU, 2014/24/EU and 2014/25/EU, and amending Regulations (EC) No 1370/2007, (EU) 2023/1542, (EU) 2024/1157, (EU) 2024/1252, (EU) 2024/1735, (EU) 2024/1781, (EU) 2024/2847, (EU) 2024/3110 and (EU) 2025/40, and Directives 2008/98/EC, (EU) 2019/882, (EU) 2022/2381, (EU) 2023/1791 and (EU) 2024/1760 (Public Procurement Act)

open on 5 Oct 2026 Open for answers from 15 Sep 2026 to 30 Nov 2026 (open). Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Single market.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission proposes a revision of EU public procurement rules through a new regulation that repeals the existing 2014 directives on public procurement, utilities, and concessions. The initiative aims to modernize and simplify rules, reduce administrative burdens, and enable preferences for European products in strategic sectors to secure supply chains and add EU value. It also amends multiple sector-specific regulations and directives containing procurement provisions to improve coherence and transparency. Furthermore, the proposal addresses high administrative burdens and legal uncertainty identified in previous evaluations regarding public buyers and businesses.

  • Revises and replaces Directives 2014/23/EU, 2014/24/EU, and 2014/25/EU with a single Public Procurement Act regulation.
  • Enables preference for European products in strategic sectors to ensure security of supply and EU added value.
  • Aims to modernize and simplify rules, specifically considering EU start-ups and innovators.
  • Amends various sector-specific regulations and directives that contain public procurement provisions.
  • Addresses administrative burdens and legal uncertainty that currently affect public buyers and businesses, including SMEs.

Affected, as named in the text: Public authorities; EU business; SMEs; Entities operating in specific sectors (water, energy, transport, and postal services); EU start-ups and innovators.

Dossier summary from the portal

In the Political Guidelines 2024-2029 for the next European Commission, the President Ursula von der Leyen announced a revision of the public procurement directives. This revision will enable preference to be given to European products in public procurement, helping to ensure EU added value and secure the supply of vital technologies, products, and services. It will also modernise and simplify procurement rules, while making public procurement a tool for strategic investment.
