Chemicals regulation - update of EU rules for test methods

Full official titleCOMMISSION REGULATION (EU) …/… amending Regulation (EC) No 440/2008 as regards the test methods, to adapt them to technical progress

closed Open for answers from 29 Sep 2026 to 27 Oct 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

This draft Commission regulation amends Regulation (EC) No 440/2008 to adapt chemical test methods to technical progress. It incorporates new and updated OECD test guidelines into EU rules to generate information on physicochemical, toxicological, and ecotoxicological properties. The initiative aims to reduce the number of animals used for experimental purposes in accordance with Directive 2010/63/EU.

  • Updates the list of approved methods for testing chemicals under Regulation (EC) No 440/2008.
  • Introduces one new test method and updates four existing test methods for ecotoxicity.
  • Introduces eighteen updated test methods for human health effects, including ten in vitro tests.
  • Replaces outdated full descriptions with links to the latest versions of corresponding OECD Test Guidelines.
  • Aims to reduce and refine animal testing for chemicals.

Dossier summary from the portal

The EU’s REACH Regulation places controls on chemicals that may be potentially hazardous for humans, animals or the environment. One of its accompanying acts - Regulation 440/2008 - lists the approved methods for testing chemicals. This initiative will add a number of new/updated methods to that list, which have the potential to reduce and refine animal testing using chemicals.
