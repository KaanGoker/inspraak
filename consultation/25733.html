Rules for the calculation and verification of recycled content in plastic packaging

Full official titleCommission Implementing Decision (EU) XX of XX 2026 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) 2025/40 of the European Parliament and of the Council for the purpose of establishing the methodology for the calculation and verification of the percentage of recycled content in plastic packaging recovered from post-consumer plastic waste recycled and collected within the Union

closed Open for answers from 14 Aug 2026 to 16 Sep 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission is preparing three acts under Article 7 of Regulation (EU) 2025/40 on packaging and packaging waste, comprising two implementing acts and one delegated act scheduled for Q4 2026. The measures will establish uniform methodologies for calculating and verifying recycled content recovered from post-consumer plastic waste collected and recycled in the EU, define sustainability criteria for plastic recycling technologies, and set rules for assessing equivalence for imported plastic recyclates from non-EU countries. The initiative aims to provide legal clarity, support circularity targets, and ensure consistent verification and fair competition across the single market.

  • Establishes a calculation and verification methodology for post-consumer recycled plastic content within the EU under Article 7(8).
  • Defines the required format for technical documentation, chain of custody, and third-party audit requirements.
  • Sets sustainability criteria for plastic recycling technologies under Article 7(9) based on environmental and economic performance.
  • Establishes an equivalence methodology under Article 7(10) for verifying recycled content collected or recycled in third countries.
  • Aims to prevent divergent national practices and ensure a level playing field between EU and non-EU supply chains.
  • Targets adoption of two implementing acts and one delegated act by 31 December 2026.

Affected, as named in the text: Recyclers; Producers of recycled plastics; Converters; Packaging manufacturers; Brand owners; Importers; Distributors; Fulfilment service providers; Producers placing packaged products on the EU market; SMEs; Operators in third-country supply chains; Scientific researchers, academic organisations, learned societies and scientific associations.

What is asked: Stakeholders and the public are invited to provide views on the Commission's understanding of the problem and possible solutions, and to share relevant information. Scientific experts and organisations are invited to submit published and pre-print research, analyses, and data on recycling technologies, calculation, verification, and international supply chains. The Commission specifically requests data on estimated annual volumes of recycled content imported into the EU by polymer type and the proportion of plastic packaging placed on the EU market that originates from third countries.
