Revision of the EIT Regulation and of the Strategic Innovation Agenda

Proposal for a Regulation amending Regulation (EU) 2021/819 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2021 on the European Institute of Innovation and Technology

closed call for evidence, Ares(2026)7759770, EAC. Feedback period 12 Aug 2026 to 23 Sep 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Business and industry, Education and training, Research and innovation.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission proposes to revise the European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT) Regulation alongside a Decision establishing the EIT Strategic Innovation Agenda for the 2028-2034 period. The initiative aims to address structural challenges in the EU innovation landscape, including fragmentation, barriers to translating research into commercial ventures, funding gaps, and regional disparities. It seeks to make the EIT more flexible, impact-driven, and aligned with EU priorities by tackling the financial sustainability and governance of Knowledge and Innovation Communities (KICs). Additionally, the proposal aims to integrate the EIT into the innovation ecosystems component of Horizon Europe and avoid overlaps with other EU instruments.

  • Revises the EIT Regulation and sets out the Strategic Innovation Agenda for 2028-2034 under Article 173 TFEU.
  • Aims to enhance the financial sustainability of KICs, ensure best value for money, and address self-sustainability conditions.
  • Seeks to reduce administrative complexity in the EIT and KIC set-up while addressing governance challenges across education, business, and research.
  • Ensures compliance with EU financial and ethical rules through comitology, integration under the Horizon work programme, and stronger EIT oversight.
  • Clarifies the EIT's role to complement other EU instruments such as the European Innovation Council, the future European Competitiveness Fund, and joint undertakings.
  • Focuses KIC activities on early-stage innovation, testbeds, living labs, start-up creation, and deeper integration of the knowledge triangle.

Affected, as named in the text: public; stakeholders; academia; researchers; innovators; entrepreneurs; knowledge and innovation communities (KICs); universities; corporations; risk-capital providers; start-ups; scale-ups; Member States.

What is asked: Views on the Commission's understanding of the problem and possible solutions, any relevant information stakeholders may have, and information about the current challenges faced by the EIT and its KICs.
