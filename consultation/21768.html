Biotech Act

Full official titleProposal for a REGULATION OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL on establishing a framework of measures for strengthening Union’s biotechnology and biomanufacturing sectors particularly in the area of health and amending Regulations (EC) No 178/2002, (EC) No 1394/2007, (EU) No 536/2014, (EU) 2019/6, (EU) 2024/795 and (EU) 2024/1938 (European Biotech Act)

closed Open for answers from 19 Dec 2025 to 5 Aug 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Food safety, Public health.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission proposes a regulation establishing a framework of measures to strengthen the Union's biotechnology and biomanufacturing sectors, particularly in the areas of health, food, and feed. The initiative establishes recognition and support frameworks for health biotechnology strategic projects and high-impact strategic projects to expand industrial capacity and investment. It also introduces biosecurity definitions and control criteria for benchtop nucleic acid synthesis equipment and synthetic sequences of concern. In addition, the proposal amends several existing Union legislative acts, including clinical trial application and dossier rules under Regulation (EU) No 536/2014, to simplify regulatory procedures and accelerate time-to-market.

  • Establishes a support framework for health biotechnology strategic projects and high-impact strategic projects to mobilize investment and accelerate permitting.
  • Defines biotechnology products of concern, covering benchtop nucleic acid synthesis equipment for de novo synthesis.
  • Sets criteria for sequences of concern, targeting synthetic de novo nucleic acid sequences of at least 50 nucleotides or amino acid sequences of at least 17 amino acids matching listed dangerous agents or pathogenicity risks.
  • Amends Annex I to Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 to update clinical trial dossier requirements, cover letters, and protocol rules.
  • Requires clinical trial cover letters to detail decentralized activities conducted outside traditional sites using digital technologies, remote procedures, and alternative delivery models.
  • Amends Regulations (EC) No 178/2002, (EC) No 1394/2007, (EU) No 536/2014, (EU) 2019/6, (EU) 2024/795, and (EU) 2024/1938 to streamline administrative procedures.

Affected, as named in the text: clinical trial sponsors; economic operators supplying synthetic nucleic acids; customers purchasing synthetic nucleic acids; individual users, laboratories or facilities using nucleic acid synthesis equipment; strategic project promoters and collaborators in networks and clusters; Member States.

Dossier summary from the portal

The biotech sector is research-driven, fast-moving and requires substantial and continuous public and private investments. Biotechnology is an enabling technology, which plays a critical role in several areas of the EU economy. The Biotech Act will propose a series of measures aiming at creating an enabling environment to accelerate the transition of biotech products and applications from the laboratory to factory and to the market while maintaining the highest safety standards for the protection of the population and the environment.
