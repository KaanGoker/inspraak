Amendment of protective measures against pests of plants

Full official titleCOMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) …/... amending Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/2072 as regards combined nomenclature (CN) codes

closed Open for answers from 24 Sep 2026 to 22 Oct 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Food safety.

What the Commission proposes

This draft Commission implementing regulation amends Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/2072 to update Combined Nomenclature (CN) codes across several annexes. The changes align the phytosanitary rules with the latest revisions of the customs nomenclature and reflect updated risk assessments and classification practices. Specifically, the regulation removes certain CN codes for wood veneer sheets and picture frame mouldings following updated evaluations and erroneous inclusions. It also introduces specific adjustments for wood of Betula from the US and Canada, used agricultural machinery, and various plant products.

  • Updates Combined Nomenclature codes in Annexes VI, VII, X, and XI of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/2072.
  • Removes CN codes for wood veneer sheets of a thickness of 5 mm or less where no fungal pest risk is identified.
  • Deletes erroneously added CN codes relating to mouldings for frames for pictures and photographs.
  • Updates requirements and codes for wood of Betula originating in parts of Canada and the United States.
  • Revises CN codes for used agricultural machinery and certain root and tuber vegetables based on updated customs practices.

Affected, as named in the text: National plant protection organisation; Traders of plants, plant products, and other objects; Importers of wood and agricultural machinery.

Dossier summary from the portal

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/2072 sets out protective measures against pests of plants. It includes the establishment of protected zones, prohibitions and requirements for import and trade of certain plants. Those measures are based on risk assessments carried out by EFSA, the European Plant Protection Organisation (EPPO) or other authorities. This draft act will update those protective measures, in view of the overall development of the phytosanitary risk in the EU
