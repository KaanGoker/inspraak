Amending RTS on buy-back programmes and stabilisation measures

Full official titleCOMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) …/... amending the regulatory technical standards laid down in Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1052 as regards the conditions applicable to buy-back programmes and stabilisation measures

closed Open for answers from 21 Sep 2026 to 19 Oct 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Banking and financial services.

What the Commission proposes

This European Commission consultation document proposes amendments to the regulatory technical standards laid down in Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1052 concerning buy-back programmes and stabilisation measures. The initiative aligns the regulatory framework with changes introduced by the Listing Act under Regulation (EU) 2024/2809. It aims to reduce administrative burdens for market participants by permitting the reporting and public disclosure of buy-back transactions and stabilisation measures in aggregated form. Additionally, it streamlines reporting requirements so that transactions are reported only to the competent authority of the most relevant market in terms of liquidity.

  • Allows issuers to report buy-back transactions in aggregated form only to the competent authority of the most relevant market in terms of liquidity.
  • Updates the adequate disclosure of buy-back transactions to require disclosure in aggregate form.
  • Aligns the disclosure and reporting regime for stabilisation measures with that of buy-back programmes.
  • Permits reporting and public disclosure of stabilisation measures in aggregated form.
  • Requires reporting of stabilisation measures only to the competent authority of the most relevant market in terms of liquidity, with an obligation to forward information to other trading venue authorities upon request.
  • Reflects the amendments made to Article 5 of the Market Abuse Regulation by the Listing Act.

Affected, as named in the text: issuers; offerors; entities undertaking stabilisation measures; competent authorities.

What is asked: The Commission asks respondents to provide feedback on the draft delegated regulation during the four-week feedback period.
