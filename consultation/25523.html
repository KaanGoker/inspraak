Individual learning accounts Evaluation

Evaluation of the implementation of the Council Recommendation on Individual learning accounts (2022/C 243/03)

closed cfe evl fc, Ares(2026)920415, EMPL. Feedback period 23 Jul 2026 to 18 Sep 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Employment and social affairs.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission is evaluating the implementation of the Council Recommendation on individual learning accounts adopted on 16 June 2022. The evaluation covers all 27 Member States from June 2022 to December 2026, assessing the initiative against the criteria of effectiveness, efficiency, coherence, relevance, and EU added value. In line with the Council Recommendation's requirement, the Commission will report on progress to the Council by 16 June 2027. No public consultation is planned; instead, evidence will be gathered through this Call for Evidence alongside targeted interviews, surveys, and workshops conducted by an external contractor.

  • Evaluates progress under the Council Recommendation on individual learning accounts across all 27 Member States for the period June 2022 to December 2026.
  • Assesses implementation against five standard criteria: effectiveness, efficiency, coherence, relevance, and EU added value.
  • Serves to prepare the Commission's report to the Council due by 16 June 2027.
  • No open public consultation will take place; input will be collected via this Call for Evidence and targeted consultation activities.
  • Consultation methods include semi-structured interviews (Q2-Q3 2026), targeted surveys (Q2-Q4 2026), a thematic workshop (Q4 2026), and a validation workshop (Q1 2027).
  • Evidence collection will draw on impact assessments, EU datasets (AES, LFS), Eurobarometer findings, national reports, and an external contractor study.

Affected, as named in the text: working-age adults; adult learners; Member States; national authorities; ministries responsible for employment, education and ILA schemes; managing authorities of EU funding; regional authorities; social partners; education and training providers; SMEs/associations; EU institutions and agencies; international organisations; network/umbrella organisations.

What is asked: The Commission invites interested stakeholders to submit their views, evidence, data, or analyses on the implementation of individual learning accounts, specifically addressing the Commission's understanding of the problem, possible solutions, the Recommendation's effectiveness, and potential room for improvement.

Dossier summary from the portal

The Council Recommendation on Individual Learning Accounts requires the Commission to assess progress and report to the Council by 16 June 2027. The evaluation will review efforts to enable more working-age adults to engage in training to increase adult learning participation and reduce skills gaps, in particular by supporting them in accessing labour-market relevant training and increasing their incentives to reskill and upskill. This supports the 2030 target of 60% of adults training annually.
