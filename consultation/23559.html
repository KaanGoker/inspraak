Revision of EU rules on tobacco products and tobacco advertising

Full official titleProposal for a Directive of the European Parliament and of the Council on the approximation of the laws, regulations and administrative provisions of the Member States concerning the manufacture, presentation, advertising and sale of tobacco and related products.

closed Open for answers from 18 May 2026 to 15 Jun 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Public health.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission has published a call for evidence for an impact assessment concerning the revision of EU rules on tobacco products and tobacco advertising. The initiative intends to update the regulatory framework to address recent market developments, consumption trends, and digital marketing practices, while strengthening public health protection and internal market functioning. Planned policy options include extending the legislative scope to novel products such as nicotine pouches and heated herbal products, introducing rules on flavours and disposable e-cigarettes, and reinforcing labelling and digital advertising provisions. An indicative timetable sets the legislative initiative for December 2026, supported by an impact assessment and public consultation.

  • Extend the scope of the legislation to cover novel products such as nicotine pouches and heated herbal products, with added flexibility for rapid product developments.
  • Introduce rules regarding flavours in e-cigarettes, disposable e-cigarettes, tobacco heating devices, and electronic non-nicotine delivery systems.
  • Strengthen existing provisions on labelling and packaging, including plain packaging requirements.
  • Reinforce rules governing digital marketing and advertising, particularly across digital channels and social media.
  • Introduce further requirements for products currently covered while simplifying reporting provisions to reduce administrative burden.
  • Prepare an impact assessment supported by external expertise and conduct a 12-week public consultation alongside targeted consultations.

Affected, as named in the text: citizens; businesses; economic operators; representative organisations; EU countries' public authorities; academics; researchers; non-governmental organisations; young people.

What is asked: Respondents are invited to provide feedback on the Commission's understanding of the problem and possible solutions, suggest possible changes to the current rules, express views on those rules, and share relevant information including evidence on the possible impacts of the different options.

Dossier summary from the portal

The initiative intends to update the EU tobacco control rules in response to the latest market developments, consumption trends and digital marketing practices. It aims to improve the functioning of the internal market, strengthen public health protection, and ensure the continued implementation of the World Health Organisation Framework Convention on Tobacco Control, in line with the objectives of Europe’s Beating Cancer Plan.
