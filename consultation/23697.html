Revision of EU rules on tobacco products and tobacco advertising

Full official titlePublic consultation questionnaire Revision of the EU rules on tobacco products and tobacco advertising

closed Open for answers from 22 May 2026 to 14 Aug 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Public health.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission intends to update the EU tobacco control rules to address recent market developments, consumption trends, and digital marketing practices. This initiative aims to improve the functioning of the internal market and strengthen the protection of public health across the EU. It also seeks to ensure the continued implementation of the World Health Organisation Framework Convention on Tobacco Control. Furthermore, the planned revision aligns with the overarching objectives established under Europe's Beating Cancer Plan.

  • Update EU tobacco control rules covering tobacco products and advertising.
  • Respond to recent market developments and evolving consumption trends.
  • Address challenges stemming from digital marketing practices.
  • Improve the functioning of the internal market.
  • Strengthen public health protection across the European Union.
  • Ensure continued implementation of the WHO Framework Convention on Tobacco Control in line with Europe's Beating Cancer Plan.

What is asked: Respondents are invited to provide views on the revision of EU rules on tobacco products and tobacco advertising via the public consultation questionnaire.
