Revision of the Network Code on Requirements for Grid Connection of Generators

Full official titleCOMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) …/... establishing a network code on requirements for grid connection of generators and repealing Regulation (EU) 2016/631

closed Open for answers from 8 Jul 2026 to 25 Aug 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Energy.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission proposes a draft Delegated Regulation to establish an updated network code on requirements for grid connection of generators, repealing Regulation (EU) 2016/631. The initiative extends technical connection requirements to emerging technologies, specifically electricity storage modules and vehicle-to-grid (V2G) electric vehicles combined with new supply equipment. It introduces additional technical capabilities for small-scale Type A power-generating modules, including fault-ride-through, voltage control, and frequency response. Unless otherwise specified, the new requirements are set to apply two years after the regulation enters into force.

  • Repeals Regulation (EU) 2016/631 and establishes an updated network code for connecting power-generating facilities to the interconnected system.
  • Brings electricity storage modules under the same grid connection requirements as power-generating modules, unless specific rules are required.
  • Establishes harmonised grid connection rules for vehicle-to-grid (V2G) electric vehicles and new associated electric vehicle supply equipment.
  • Extends requirements previously limited to higher categories (Types B and C) to Type A modules, including fault-ride-through, high-voltage ride through, voltage control, and frequency response.
  • Allows relevant system operators, in coordination with transmission system operators, to specify technical rules for equipment such as synchronous compensators, flywheels, and regenerative braking systems.
  • Sets a general transitional application date of two years after entry into force, while existing requirements under Regulation (EU) 2016/631 continue to apply in the interim.

Affected, as named in the text: equipment manufacturers; power-generating facility owners; system operators; transmission system operators (TSOs); regulatory authorities; electricity undertakings; consumers.

Dossier summary from the portal

The Network Codes on Requirements for Grid Connection of Generators and on Demand Connection contain binding rules to govern the connection requirements to the electricity grid. They are crucial for ensuring the safety of system operation and the efficiency of the European Union's power grid. The aim of these amendments is to update the Network Codes and future proof them to further support the EU power grid embracing emerging developments such as e-mobility, battery storage, heat pumps and energy communities.
