Green-listing certain waste for the purposes of shipments to recovery between Member States

Full official titleCOMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) …/... Amending Regulation (EU) 2024/1157 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards wastes and mixtures of wastes listed in Annex III, Annex IIIA, Annex IIIB, and Annex IV

closed Open for answers from 18 Aug 2026 to 15 Sep 2026. Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Environment.

What the Commission proposes

The draft delegated act amends Regulation (EU) 2024/1157 to update the lists of non-hazardous wastes and waste mixtures eligible for shipment between Member States under the general information requirements. It deletes temporary non-hazardous electrical and electronic waste entries GC010 and GC020 from Annex III with effect from 1 January 2027 following changes to the Basel Convention. The text adds mixtures of textile waste with footwear, hats, belts, and other accessories to Annex IIIA, and introduces five new entries into Annex IIIB for composite polymers, construction metal scrap, mattresses, permanent magnets, and vehicle metal waste. In addition, it establishes a 10% maximum contamination threshold by weight for shipments of non-hazardous glass waste under Annex III.

  • Deletes references to entries GC010 and GC020 from Annex III, ending their application from 1 January 2027.
  • Adds mixtures of textile waste classified under entry B3030 mixed with footwear, hats, belts, and other accessories to Annex IIIA.
  • Introduces new Annex IIIB entries for fibre-reinforced polymer composites (BEU06), construction aluminium and steel waste (BEU07), non-hazardous mattresses (BEU08), permanent magnets (BEU10), and vehicle metal waste (BEU11).
  • Sets a maximum contamination threshold of 10% by weight of the consignment for non-hazardous glass waste under Annex III entry B2020.
  • Allows green-listed waste to be shipped for recovery within the EU without prior written notification and consent from authorities.

Affected, as named in the text: operators; Member States; stakeholders; experts designated by each Member State.

What is asked: Feedback on the draft delegated act published on the Better Regulation Portal, specifically concerning the waste streams to be addressed and considered as green-listed for shipments between Member States.

Dossier summary from the portal

The act implements the new Waste Shipment Regulation and complements the objectives of the future Circular Economy Act. It will consider certain waste as green-listed, and possibility introduce contamination thresholds for considering sone waste as green-listed. Adoption of the act will be preceded by a consultation processed to gather input from a wide range of stakeholders on waste streams to be addressed.
