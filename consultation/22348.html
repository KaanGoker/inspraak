Digital Networks Act

Proposal for a REGULATION OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL on digital networks, amending Regulation (EU) 2015/2120, Directive 2002/58/EC and Decision No 676/2002/EC and repealing Regulation (EU) 2018/1971, Directive (EU) 2018/1972 and Decision No 243/2012/EU (Digital Networks Act)

closed prop reg, Ares(2025)4305261, CNECT. Feedback period 16 Feb 2026 to 15 Jun 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Digital economy and society.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission proposes a regulation on digital networks to establish a modernized and simplified legal framework supporting the transition from legacy infrastructure to fibre, high-quality 5G and 6G networks, and cloud-based infrastructures. The initiative aims to improve access to secure, fast, and reliable connectivity across the EU, including rural and remote regions, in line with priorities set out in the Letta, Draghi, and Niinistö reports. It seeks to incentivise investment, foster cross-border operation and service provision at scale, and adapt the regulatory framework as electronic communications evolve into digital networks. The proposal also amends Regulation (EU) 2015/2120, Directive 2002/58/EC, and Decision No 676/2002/EC, while repealing Regulation (EU) 2018/1971, Directive (EU) 2018/1972, and Decision No 243/2012/EU.

  • Promote the transition from legacy copper networks to fibre, 5G/6G, and cloud-based infrastructures.
  • Put in place rules that facilitate investment in modern, high-speed broadband and digital networks.
  • Support the Digital Decade targets of universal gigabit connectivity and 5G in populated areas by 2030.
  • Enable cross-border service provision and increased scale across the EU Single Market.
  • Merge and update existing legal frameworks into a single regulation on digital networks.
  • Address connectivity needs for advanced technologies, including artificial intelligence, cloud, and edge computing.

Affected, as named in the text: public authorities; businesses; citizens; end-users.

Dossier summary from the portal

The initiative aims at improving access to secure, fast, and reliable connectivity for the transition towards cloud-based infrastructure and Artificial Intelligence. To this end, it aims to help boost secure high-speed broadband, both fixed and wireless, and incentivise and encourage investments in digital infrastructure. It builds on the Commission’s White Paper: “How to master Europe’s digital infrastructure needs” adopted on 21 February 2024.
