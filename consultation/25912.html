VAT and circular economy

Full official titleVAT and the Circular Economy

open on 5 Oct 2026 Open for answers from 9 Sep 2026 to 4 Nov 2026 (open). Answer on the Commission's site. Topics: Taxation.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission has launched a consultation on VAT and the circular economy to support the transition towards sustainable consumption. The initiative focuses on modernising value-added tax rules that potentially hinder circular business models and secondary markets. Key areas of review include the VAT treatment of second-hand goods, circular production chains, the destruction of viable goods, and VAT deduction rules for passenger cars. Any proposed solutions aim to remain proportionate, fraud-proof, and fully compatible with the common EU VAT system.

  • Modernising VAT rules to remove barriers to circular business models
  • Reviewing the VAT treatment of second-hand goods
  • Examining VAT rules for circular production chains
  • Addressing the VAT implications of the destruction of viable goods
  • Assessing VAT deduction rules for passenger cars
  • Ensuring changes remain proportionate and fraud-proof

Affected, as named in the text: Businesses involved in circular economy models; Second-hand goods traders; Consumers choosing sustainable options; Tax authorities.

What is asked: Respondents are asked to comment on how current VAT rules affect circular business models, second-hand goods, circular production chains, the destruction of viable goods, and passenger car deductions, and how these rules should be modernised.

Dossier summary from the portal

This initiative supports the transition towards a circular economy by modernising selected VAT rules that may currently hinder circular business models and sustainable consumption choices. It will focus on the VAT treatment of second-hand goods and circular production chains, the destruction of viable goods, and VAT deduction rules for passenger cars. The initiative will ensure that any solutions remain proportionate, fraud-proof and compatible with the principles and structure of the common VAT system.
