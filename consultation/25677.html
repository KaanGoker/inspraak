Delegated act to extend derogation for non-organic protein feed

COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) …/... amending Annex II to Regulation (EU) 2018/848 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the authorisation to use non-organic protein feed in the nutrition of piglets and young poultry

closed reg del draft, Ares(2026)3680075, AGRI. Feedback period 3 Aug 2026 to 31 Aug 2026. Initiative on the portal. Topics: Agriculture and rural development.

What the Commission proposes

The European Commission proposes to amend Annex II to Regulation (EU) 2018/848 to extend the authorisation for using non-organic protein feed in the diets of piglets and young poultry until 31 December 2036. Under the draft delegated act, Member State competent authorities may continue authorising a maximum of 5% non-organic protein feed per 12-month period for piglets up to 35 kg and young poultry when organic protein feed is unavailable in sufficient quantity. The extension is based on Member State data from 2023, 2024, and 2025 confirming a structural shortage of organic protein feed across the Union market. The measure aims to cover essential amino acids required for the growth and welfare of these young monogastric animals while long-term production and processing capacities are developed.

  • Extends the authorisation allowing up to 5% non-organic protein feed for piglets up to 35 kg and young poultry until 31 December 2036.
  • Maintains the condition that farmers must be unable to source organic feed and competent authorities must confirm its non-availability.
  • Requires non-organic protein feed to be produced or prepared without chemical solvents and to provide specific protein compounds.
  • Cites reporting from Member States showing a persistent structural shortage of organic protein feed on the Union market.
  • Identifies that scaling organic protein feed requires significant time for land conversion, processing infrastructure, and certification, alongside risks from adverse climatic events.

Affected, as named in the text: Farmers; Competent authorities of Member States; Piglets of up to 35 kg; Young poultry; WTO partners; Group of Experts on Organic Production.

Dossier summary from the portal

The delegated act prolongs the possibility for Member States to authorise up to 5% of non organic protein feed for the nutrition of organic young poultry and porcine animals to ensure that their needs in specific amino acids are covered during their growth.
