Multi-agent system for EU public affairs

Which open Commission consultations should your organisation answer?

Inspraak reads an organisation's EU Transparency Register profile, finds the open Have Your Say consultations that fit it, and shows who already answered, who met the Commissioners' cabinets on the subject and what the proposal says, with every figure linked to its public source. Seven agents do the work under a supervisor, and a person decides before anything is drafted.

How a run works

  1. Profilerreads the register entry and past submissions
  2. Matcherranks open consultations, one model rerank
  3. Landscapewho already answered, verified quotes
  4. Lobby mappercabinet meetings on the subject
  5. Readerthe proposal, cited page by page
  6. Human go / no-gonothing is drafted without it
  7. Draftera 4,000 character response
  8. Criticremoves every sentence it cannot source

Supervisor routes between the agents on what the run already holds, and a second human review closes the run. LangGraph with a Postgres checkpointer, so a run can wait days for a decision. {% if static_mode %}This snapshot shows the first five agents' output; drafts stay in the local app. {% endif %}Method and evaluation
